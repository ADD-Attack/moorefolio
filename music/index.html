Music — Visualizer Prototypes

Six takes on the same idea, built by Opus (medium) to different influence mixes. Pick the direction; the winner becomes the player for the songs on this site.

Every prototype opens with the same demo loaded: Mountain King v1 — just press play. Each also accepts any song you drag onto the page. Because we make the music, these don't have to guess: the demo ships with a score sidecar (demo.viz.json: 160 exact kick hits, 550 melody notes, terrace sections, generated from the composition script), so in SCORE mode the visuals fire on the composed notes, not an FFT guess. Drop a plain MP3 with no sidecar and they fall back to FFT mode. Dancers also reads a dance guide (demo.dance.json) — who holds the stage, the feeling, and the palette for each stretch of the song.

Trippy Flow

A mirrored fluid — domain-warped noise through a rotating kaleidoscope. Hue drifts with the spectral centroid, bass swells the metaballs, every kick sends a ripple. Nothing flashes.

Aurora

A night sky over a still lake. Three aurora curtains and their reflection; each melody note rises to a height set by its pitch and joins a fading constellation. The kick just makes the horizon breathe.

Beautiful 5 · Colorful 3 · Trippy 2 · Intense 0

Signal Lab

The instrument panel: log spectrum, oscilloscope, dB meters, BPM and section readouts, a pitch roll — and three lamps for low/mid/high. The low lamp fires on the kick, like the Raspberry Pi.

Technical 5 · Beautiful 2

Dancers

Seven geometric characters who choreograph: crouch in anticipation, hop on the kick, spin on the hats, re-form at each section. With the dance guide, the named lead takes the stage and the palette follows the song's intent.

Dancing 5 · Colorful 4 · Intense 3

Overdrive

A reactor core that charges with the song. Kick = particle burst + shockwave ring; sustained loudness fills a charge battery until the visual latches into a climax state, so the climax looks like one.

Intense 5 · Colorful 5 · Trippy 3

Loom — wild card

The builder's own invention: “every song leaves a cloth.” The track is woven top to bottom into a textile — kick beats the reed, melody is embroidered stitch by stitch, and the finished song remains as cloth.
