Services & prices

Three jobs. Three numbers.

Each number is a starting figure. “From” means from. Ray quotes the final price in person, before any work starts.

Gutter cleaning

from $120 single-story house

The $120 starting figure is for a single-story house; a two-story house is quoted in person.

Gutter-guard fitting

from $6 per linear foot

Fitting only. Ray installs guards you already have, or guards he supplies at the quoted price.

Roof patching

from $180 quoted after he sees the leak

Small leaks, and replacing up to a dozen missing or damaged shingles per visit.

What Ray turns away

Said as plainly as the prices.

  • Nothing over two stories. He will not take the job.
  • No emergency call-outs. Booked work only.
  • Nothing inside four days. Book at least 4 days ahead.
  • No weekends. Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
  • No cards. Cash or check only.
  • No full roofs or tear-offs.
  • No new gutter installation.
  • Nowhere outside the six towns: Kansas City MO, Independence, Raytown, Grandview, Liberty, Gladstone.
