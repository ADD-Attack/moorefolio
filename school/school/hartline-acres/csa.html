How the season works

  • Two pickup points, every weekThe farm on Wednesdays, or the River Market stand on Saturdays. Week 1 is Wednesday, June 3 or Saturday, June 6, 2026; week 20 is Wednesday, October 14 or Saturday, October 17.
  • Pickup onlyEvery box is collected at one of the two pickup points.
  • Full share $28 a week · half share $16 a weekTake 1 or 2 shares. You pay for the weeks from your start week through week 20.
  • Check or cash, at your first pickupThose are the only two ways to pay for the CSA.
  • Skip a week if you need toA skipped box is donated. There are no refunds for skips.
