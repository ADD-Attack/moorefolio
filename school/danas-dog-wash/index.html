The van's rules

Four towns

Kansas City, Independence, Raytown and Lee's Summit. Outside those, the van route doesn't work — Dana can't come.

90 lbs, tops

The table maxes out at 90 lbs. Small is under 25, medium is 25 to 60, large is over 60.

Dogs only

Dana used to groom cats. She stopped. Too many scratches.
