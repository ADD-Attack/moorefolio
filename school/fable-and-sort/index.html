Fable & Sort

Letterpress · the West Bottoms, Kansas City, Missouri

No. 1 · The shop

One colour.
One sheet.
One printer.

Fable & Sort is Ruth Calloway's letterpress shop. She sets the type by hand and runs both presses herself: a 1911 Chandler & Price treadle press and a Vandercook proof press. Everything is printed on cotton paper.

No. 2 · What the shop prints

Three kinds of work. Nothing else.

  1. Wedding & event invitations

    from $340 for 100

    4 weeks from approved proof

  2. Business cards & stationery

    from $85 for 100

    2 weeks from approved proof

  3. Posters & broadsides

    from $190 for 25

    3 weeks from approved proof

All on cotton paper. Rush work is not offered.

No. 3 · How a job moves

Set. Proof. Print.

  1. I. Set by hand

    Type is set by hand from the shop's type cases.

  2. II. Proof, then approve

    A proof is pulled on the Vandercook and approved before the run.

  3. III. One colour, one sheet

    Printing is one colour at a time, one sheet at a time, on the 1911 Chandler & Price.
