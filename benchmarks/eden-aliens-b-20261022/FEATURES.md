# Frozen feature list — EDEN-3D-01 (set by Forge 2026-10-22, frozen at checkpoint 1)

Amended from the producer proposal against aliens-b-3d-reference.png (visible local forms the proposal omitted are added, Decision 35.3).

- **F1 Mouth / lip ring:** a thick torus lip with a bean/kidney rim (pinched inward at the upper front). It projects forward of the body. The lower lip juts further than the upper.
- **F1b Mouth interior:** a deep cavity that narrows inward, modeled not painted. The inner wall shows a step inside the lip.
- **F1c Peri-oral collar:** a fleshy band 2–3 deep wrapping the lip ring where it meets the shell, separated by seams that have volume.
- **F2 Dorsal plate bands:** 3–4 overlapping transverse shell bands over the top and back, front to rear. Each is domed, and each overhangs the next at a deep seam. The front band splits into smaller plates near the mouth.
- **F2b Flank plate row:** a lower row of large plates on the mid-flank, below the dorsal bands, divided by deep vertical-to-diagonal seams.
- **F3 Belly mass:** a smooth, unplated egg-shaped underside. It is the widest volume, and the plates hang over it.
- **F4 Legs ×4:** short and splayed, each in two parts: a domed shoulder pad growing out of the body, with a crease, then a tapered rounded foot angled outward.
- **F5 Tail:** short and tapering at the rear, with 6–7 stacked ring segments. It curves slightly upward and ends in a rounded tip.
- **S Silhouette:** in side view, an egg body longer than it is tall, with the mouth bulging out of the front-upper quadrant, the tail low at the rear, and the legs set inside the body footprint.
