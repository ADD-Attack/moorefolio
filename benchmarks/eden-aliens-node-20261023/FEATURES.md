# Frozen feature list — EDEN-3D-02 aliens_node (frozen at Checkpoint 1, 2026-10-23)

These are written as forms. MATCH is judged against `alien_node_full.png` plus `ref/aliens-node-3d-reference.png`.

- **F1 Mound body.** Asymmetric bulbous mass that is wider than tall. It is broadest at mid-height, has a slightly flattened crown, and tucks in at the base where the roots spring out. The surface between the pods is lumpy, made of swollen sub-lobes (for example the large bare lobe right of center on the front).
- **F2 Pod field.** Raised domed lenses, each seated in its own thick, rolled rim collar.
  - One large front-center oval, its long axis vertical.
  - One low-right teardrop pod. Its point runs up into a vein.
  - One pod each at the top-center, top-left and top-right; the top-left and top-right ones are big and tilt outward.
  - One flank pod on each side, at mid-height.
  - The domes stand proud of their rims, and the rims stand proud of the body.
- **F3 Horns.** Two spikes on the crown behind the top pod, one at the left-rear and one at the right-rear. Each is gnarled, tapers to a point and leans slightly outward. The left horn has a secondary prong.
- **F4 Root-foot ring.** At least 8 root feet splayed radially from the base. Each is a thick root that tapers and forks into 2–3 claw tips that touch the ground. Rigged as separate rigid nodes (≥4).
- **F5 Vein ridges (amendment).** Raised, rounded vein cords that branch between the pods and merge into the pod rims and down into the roots. The troughs between them are clean, deep and modeled, never surface noise.

F5 was added because the brief folded the veins into F1. They are a distinct, dominant local form in the reference (Decision 35.3).
