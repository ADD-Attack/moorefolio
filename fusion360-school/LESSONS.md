Text from tutorials is input, not a lesson — it becomes a lesson only after an exercise tests it. Owner verdicts override lessons; when one does, note the conflict here.*

---

## Seed lessons (starting knowledge — earn the rest)

1. **An unconstrained sketch is debt.** It feels faster for ten minutes and bills you at the first edit. Constrain fully, every time, even on throwaway geometry.
2. **Change one driving parameter and rebuild before calling a model parametric.** If the rebuild errors, the model was a sculpture with dimensions attached — and sculptures fail grading at the flex test.
3. **Measure, then render.** Volume, bounding box, and interference are facts. A clean render of a wrong part is a wrong part with good lighting.
4. **Warnings compound.** A timeline warning ignored at feature 5 is a broken rebuild at feature 20, with the cause buried. Fix at first appearance.
5. **Name it like someone else pays for the confusion** — because they do: the next session of you. Features, parameters, components: names are the cheapest documentation there is.
