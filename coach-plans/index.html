What's an API key?

An API key is a private code from a model provider. Give it to Chess Coach and your games run on your own account with that provider, billed to you there instead of to us. Keep it private, like a password. Not sure? You don't need this.

Family & privacy

Accounts belong to adults. New Player profiles store a display name only. No ads. No chat with strangers. Ever.

Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel anytime, in a couple of clicks, and your plan simply runs to the end of the period you already paid for. No fee for stopping, and you can come back later.

What happens to games I don't use?

If you skip a day, skipped games wait — up to 8 ready on Coach, 17 on Pro. A quiet week does not waste them; they are there when you sit back down.

Sol or Astra — what's the difference?

Sol is a patient coach for every level, clear and steady. Astra goes deeper: longer lines, finer points, and sharper feedback on close positions. Both play through the same engine-checked moves.

Who reviews my games?

The model your plan uses — Sol on Coach, Astra on Pro, your own model with BYOK.

Can I share it with my children?

Yes. Profiles live under one account, so each person in the house gets their own profile, games, and progress — including a New Player profile for someone just starting.

What happens to my key with BYOK?

Your keys are stored on our servers, never in your browser. Chess Coach uses your key only to run your games and reviews, and you can remove it from your account settings at any time.
