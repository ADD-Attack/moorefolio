What We Do

Repairs, installs &
everything in between

If it lifts, rolls, seals, or opens your garage door, Bill fixes it — and he’ll tell you plainly when a repair isn’t worth your money.

  • Broken Spring Repair

    Torsion springs replaced right — often the same day. It’s our most common call, and the one you shouldn’t DIY.

    Most common call

  • Cables & Pulleys

    Frayed cables and worn pulleys make a door crooked, noisy, and dangerous. We replace them before they snap.

  • Opener Install & Repair

    Chamberlain openers, quiet belt drives, and timer-to-close peace of mind — installed clean or your old unit revived.

  • Keypads, Remotes & myQ

    New keypads, extra remotes, and smart myQ setups programmed and tested before we leave your driveway.

  • Weather Stripping

    Keep the Kansas wind, rain, and critters out. Fresh seals cut drafts and quiet a rattling door.

  • Tune-Ups & Adjustments

    Balance, lubrication, track and roller checks. A yearly tune-up is the cheapest way to skip the emergency call.

  • New Garage Doors

    Single, double, and multi-door installs — measured, ordered, and hung right, priced under the big companies.

    Priced under the big guys

  • Emergency Repairs

    Door hit by a car, stuck shut with yours inside, broken at 9pm? Evenings and weekends — we answer, 24 hours a day.

    24-hour service
