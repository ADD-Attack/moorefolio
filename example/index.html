Process

Talk, see it, build, ship

A process sized for one builder and a busy contractor: few meetings, an early look at the real thing, no surprises at the end.

Talk

A real conversation about your trade, your service area, and the jobs you want more of. Christopher says plainly if he's not the right fit — and what the job would take if he is.

See It

Before the full build, you get a working preview — a live page with the booking flow running, opened on your own phone. Decisions get made looking at the thing.

Build

The site and the scheduling get built in visible stages, wired to your calendar. You see progress as it happens, and changes get discussed while they're still cheap.

Ship

It launches with a plain-English note on what was built and how to ask for changes. The person who built it is the person you email.
