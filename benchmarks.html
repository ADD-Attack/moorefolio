Godot · In-browser · One scene per model

Model benchmarks, playable.

Every benchmark here is a small playable Godot scene — one per model build — so you can feel what a model shipped instead of reading a score table. The picker lives inside the engine: choose a benchmark in the frame below and it loads right there, with a live frame-rate HUD riding along.

MooreFolio Benchmarks — Godot 4.7

How it works: the frame above is one self-contained Godot app. Its start screen is the navigator — launch a benchmark, watch the HUD, and use the ‹ Benchmarks button inside the app to come back and pick another. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Slot 01

Sprite Storm

A few hundred sprites bounce around the field, every one moved and drawn live. Crank the sprite count up or down and watch the frame time bend — a plain, honest draw-and-update load.

Slot 02

Path Grid

Forty agents A*-pathfind across an obstacle grid and repath the instant they arrive. The readout counts paths computed per second — CPU thinking, not just pixels.

New model builds land as new slots in the in-engine navigator — same container, same two tests, different author. Prototype: two benchmarks live today.
